Research questions

  • Physiological responses across life stages

    Adult activity windows versus embryonic developmental opportunity under warming and heat events.

  • Microclimate & mechanistic niches

    Building and validating animal–environment exchange models that respect shade, nests and elevation.

  • Conservation under global change

    Protected-area effectiveness, desert bird communities, forest degradation and stopover threats.
