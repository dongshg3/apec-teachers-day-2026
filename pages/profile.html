Liang Ma · 马亮

Youth Researcher (Associate Professor) · Doctoral Supervisor · Assistant Dean

School of Ecology, Sun Yat-sen University · Shenzhen Campus · maliang26@mail.sysu.edu.cn

APEC Lab PI focusing on animal physiological ecology and biodiversity conservation: how terrestrial vertebrates experience climate and land-use change across life stages, and how mechanistic models can turn those measurements into protection priorities.

36+SCI papers (Nat Commun, Current Biology, GCB…)
4National-level projects as PI

Research questions

  • Physiological responses across life stages

    Adult activity windows versus embryonic developmental opportunity under warming and heat events.

  • Microclimate & mechanistic niches

    Building and validating animal–environment exchange models that respect shade, nests and elevation.

  • Conservation under global change

    Protected-area effectiveness, desert bird communities, forest degradation and stopover threats.
