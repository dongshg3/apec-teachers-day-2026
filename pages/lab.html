Lab briefing

APEC Lab — Animal Physiological Ecology and Conservation

Experiments, mechanistic niche models and spatial analysis — wired to ask what climate change costs vertebrates, stage by stage.

School of Ecology · Sun Yat-sen University · PI: Liang Ma

Mission

APEC（动物生理生态与保护课题组）关注以气候变化为代表的环境变化对陆生脊椎动物造成的生理后果，并据此提出保护对策。研究整合控制实验、机理生态位模型与空间分析，追踪从个体能量与水分交换到分布区与保护区配置的因果链。

Focal taxa include reptiles and birds; focal stages include the often-overlooked embryo. Focal habitats span deserts, elevational gradients and forests whose canopy microclimates are themselves under pressure.

Methods the lab teaches

01 · Experiment

Controlled thermal & hydric treatments

Incubation, water loss, activity and performance assays that ground model parameters in organismal reality.

02 · Model

Mechanistic niche models

Energy and mass balance in real microclimates — including plasticity and evolutionary response where data allow.

03 · Space

Spatial conservation analysis

Range shifts, protected-area overlap, and priority surfaces that make physiology actionable for managers.

04 · Synthesis

Life-stage aware planning

When adult and embryo risk maps disagree, APEC treats the mismatch as the scientific result — not noise.
